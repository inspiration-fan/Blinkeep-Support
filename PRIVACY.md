# 隐私声明 · Privacy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-10-28

## 中文

Blinkeep 在本机处理你选择的照片、视频和人脸信息，开发者不通过应用收集或接收这些内容。人脸对应只在所选素材间辅助编辑，不建立身份库。无需 Blinkeep 账号，无广告或第三方分析，拍摄不录音。

系统照片选择器只提供选中的素材；另存相册只请求添加权限。作品及编辑素材副本可在应用内删除，不影响系统相册原件。使用与导出记录保存在本机。系统备份及 iCloud 照片由 iOS 设置管理，可能产生设备外副本。

Apple 处理购买与恢复购买，应用使用必要交易信息验证权益。主动分享的内容由你选择的接收应用处理。隐私问题请通过下方邮箱或 GitHub Issues 联系。

## English

Blinkeep processes selected photos, videos, and face information on your device. The developer does not collect or receive this content through the app. Face matching supports edits within selected material, without building an identity database. There is no Blinkeep account, advertising, or third-party analytics. Capture records no audio.

The system photo picker provides only chosen items; saving to Photos requests add-only access. Keeps and editable source copies can be deleted inside the app without deleting Photos originals. Usage and export records are stored locally. iOS settings manage backups and iCloud Photos, which may create copies outside your device.

Apple handles purchases and restores; the app uses necessary transaction information to verify access. Sharing hands content to your chosen receiving app under its policies. For privacy questions, contact us using the email or GitHub Issues below.
