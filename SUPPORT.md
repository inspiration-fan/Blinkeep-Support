# 支持说明 · Support

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-10-28

## 中文

请通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/inspiration-fan/Blinkeep-Support/issues) 反馈问题，并提供设备型号、iOS 版本、Blinkeep 版本、复现步骤及错误提示。

Issues 是公开的，请勿上传私人照片、视频、购买收据或其他敏感信息。开发者无法访问你本机的原图。退订请使用 Apple 账户的订阅设置；退款请通过 Apple 申请。

## English

Report issues through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/inspiration-fan/Blinkeep-Support/issues). Include your device model, iOS version, Blinkeep version, steps to reproduce, and any error message.

Issues are public: do not post private photos, videos, purchase receipts, or other sensitive information. The developer cannot access originals on your device. Cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account subscription settings; request refunds from Apple.
